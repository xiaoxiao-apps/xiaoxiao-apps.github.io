English

Developer: Xiaoxiao Apps

"Offer Compare" is a multi-offer net-salary comparison tool. We follow a strict "local-only, zero-collection" principle:

  • All data stays on your device. Every offer you enter (salary, bonus, social insurance and housing fund parameters) is stored in a local on-device database only. Nothing is uploaded to any server, and the developer cannot access your data. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data.
  • We collect no personal data — no name, email, location, or device identifiers.
  • No third-party data-collection SDKs. The app includes no analytics, advertising, or tracking components, and shares no data with third parties. Completely ad-free.
  • No account required. The app has no login system; all features work out of the box.
  • In-app purchase: Pro is unlocked via an Apple one-time in-app purchase of ¥6 (not a subscription). Payments are processed by Apple; we never receive your payment details. Purchases can be restored via Apple's "Restore Purchases".
  • Export & share: Comparison report images and data backups are generated and saved by you on purpose. Sharing uses the system share sheet and data never leaves your device through us.
