常见问题 FAQ

1. 照片水印是怎么加上的？ / How are watermarks added?

时间戳（以及可选的位置）水印在拍摄时直接烧入照片像素，无法被无痕篡改，配合 SHA-256 指纹可自证完整性。The timestamp (and optional location) is burned into the photo pixels at capture time; combined with the SHA-256 fingerprint, each photo's integrity is self-verifiable.

2. 催告函有法律效力吗？ / Is the demand letter legally valid?

催告函基于《民法典》相关条款（577、585、733 条）自动生成，供协商沟通使用，仅供参考，不构成法律意见。重大纠纷请咨询执业律师或向住建部门、法院求助。The demand letter is generated from Civil Code references for negotiation purposes only and is not legal advice. For serious disputes, consult a licensed lawyer or the relevant authority.

3. 数据会丢失吗？换手机怎么办？ / Will I lose my data?

所有数据存在本机。换机前使用「备份指南」导出备份文件，新设备导入即可恢复。退租前建议同时把 PDF 证据包另存一份。Export a backup before switching devices; we also recommend saving a copy of your PDF evidence pack separately.

4. Pro 内购包含什么？是订阅吗？ / What does Pro include?

Pro 为一次性买断（非订阅、无后续费用）：PDF 证据包、同机位对比拍摄、催告函生成器、双方签名交接页、短视频留证、照片防篡改哈希。免费版可完整使用留证核心功能。换设备或重装后可用「恢复购买」找回。Pro is a one-time purchase (not a subscription) unlocking PDF packs, comparison shooting, the demand-letter generator, dual signatures, video evidence, and anti-tampering hashes. Use "Restore Purchases" after reinstalling.

5. 需要联网或注册吗？ / Does it need internet or login?

不需要。核心功能完全离线，无账号体系，不收集任何个人数据。位置权限仅在你使用定位水印时按需申请。No internet and no login required; location permission is requested only for the location watermark feature.
