  • 数据全部存储在设备本地。你拍摄的照片、视频、水表电表读数、物品清单、交接签名等全部数据仅保存在你自己的设备上，不会上传到任何服务器，开发者无法访问你的数据。卸载应用将删除全部本地数据。

English

Developer: Xiaoxiao Apps

"RentCheck" is a rental evidence and deposit-protection tool. We follow a strict "local-only, zero-collection" principle:

  • All data stays on your device. Photos, videos, meter readings, item checklists, and handover signatures are stored locally only and are never uploaded to any server. The developer cannot access your data. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data.
  • We collect no personal data — no name, email, or device identifiers.
  • Location is used only on request. The photo location-watermark feature requires your explicit permission. Location is used solely to stamp your photos, is never recorded or uploaded, and can be turned off at any time.
  • No third-party data-collection SDKs. The app includes no analytics, advertising, or tracking components. Completely ad-free.
  • No account required. All features work out of the box.
  • In-app purchase: Pro is unlocked via an Apple one-time in-app purchase (not a subscription). Payments are processed by Apple; we never receive your payment details. Purchases can be restored via "Restore Purchases".
  • Export & share: PDF evidence packs and backups are generated and saved by you on purpose. SHA-256 fingerprints of photos are computed on-device only and never uploaded.
